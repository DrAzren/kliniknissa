## Items flagged for doctor review
- [ ] "Resdung" is used as the everyday word for allergic rhinitis. Confirm the wording.
- [ ] "Guna inhaler pencegah setiap hari seperti arahan doktor": correct for daily controller regimens, but some patients are on as-needed ICS-formoterol (GINA track 1). The phrase "seperti arahan doktor" covers this; doctor to accept or adjust.
- [ ] On-screen card "Paru-paru lebih terbeban" (scene #4, overweight) is a simplification added for the visual; it is not in the narration.
- [ ] "Ada empat faktor utama": other risk factors (air pollution, occupational exposure, prematurity) are not mentioned.
