# Kencing tikus (leptospirosis): semakan fakta (draf)
Sumber utama: **KKM, Guidelines for the Diagnosis, Management, Prevention and Control of Leptospirosis in Malaysia (2011)** — teks dibaca daripada salinan terbitan semula (PDF di moh.gov.my disekat, 403). Nasihat banjir KKM (kenyataan media musim banjir).

| Kenyataan dalam video | Garis panduan KKM 2011 | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Bakteria daripada air kencing tikus & haiwan lain mencemari air dan tanah | "water or soil contaminated with the urine of rodents, carrier or diseased animals" | ✅ |
| Bakteria berbentuk spiral | *Leptospira* ialah spirochaete (bakteria berpilin) | ✅ |
| Masuk melalui luka, mata, hidung, mulut | "contact through skin, mucosa/conjunctiva… Ingestion of contaminated water may also…"; kumpulan berisiko: "open skin wounds" | ✅ |
| Risiko tinggi semasa banjir / membersihkan lumpur | Pendedahan pekerjaan & rekreasi kepada air/tanah tercemar; amaran KKM musim banjir | ✅ |
| Inkubasi 2–30 hari, purata 10 hari | "usually 10 days, with a range of 2 to 30 days" | ✅ |
| Demam mengejut, sakit kepala, sakit otot terutama betis, mata merah | "Myalgia particularly associated with the calf muscles…", "Conjunctival suffusion", "Headache" | ✅ |
| Teruk: kuning, kencing berkurang, boleh maut | Weil's syndrome: "jaundice, renal failure, haemorrhage"; "oliguria"; outbreak "significant number of deaths" | ✅ |
| But getah, sarung tangan, tutup luka kalis air, mandi air bersih selepas terdedah | "Wear… rubber boots and gloves. Cover skin lesions with waterproof dressings. Wash with clean water immediately after exposure." | ✅ |
| Jangan redah/bermain air banjir | Nasihat KKM musim banjir | ✅ |
| Demam dalam sebulan selepas banjir → ke klinik, beritahu doktor | "Seek immediate medical treatment if develop symptoms within the incubation period" (≤ 30 hari) | ✅ |
| Rawatan antibiotik awal, pulih | Antibiotik (doxycycline / penisilin) — rawatan awal penting | ⚠️ semak doktor: "pulih sepenuhnya" ialah hasil cerita rekaan |
| "Ujian darah sahkan" | Diagnosis makmal (serologi MAT/IgM, PCR); rawatan dimulakan berdasarkan klinikal | ⚠️ semak doktor: dalam amalan, rawatan sering dimulakan sebelum keputusan makmal |

Nota: leptospirosis penyakit wajib dilapor (notifiable) sejak 9 Dis 2010 — tidak dimasukkan dalam video.
